Detailed Description

Backend-neutral, bounded GATT operations for one selected device.

Device, service, and characteristic identifiers are opaque to callers and are valid only for the current connection/service-tree lifetime. Blocking operations are synchronous and have finite deadlines; notification events are delivered asynchronously on the backend's documented serialized worker. Callers must keep blocking operations off UI threads and notification callbacks prompt.
