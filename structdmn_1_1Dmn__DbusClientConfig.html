std::string dmn::Dmn_DbusClientConfig::bus_address

Exact bus-daemon address, required only for Dmn_DbusBusKind::Address.

The address can identify a standard system/session bus or a separately started private bus instance. It must identify a bus daemon, not a service process listening for a direct peer-to-peer connection.

◆ bus_kind

Dmn_DbusBusKind dmn::Dmn_DbusClientConfig::bus_kind {Dmn_DbusBusKind::Session}

Selects the session bus, system bus, or explicit address.

All choices create a dedicated private connection owned by Dmn_DbusClient. The connection is private to this client; the selected system, session, or separately started private bus may be shared with other processes. Defaults to Session.
