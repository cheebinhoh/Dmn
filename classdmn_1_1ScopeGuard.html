Detailed Description

template<typename F>
requires std::is_nothrow_invocable_v<F &>
class dmn::ScopeGuard< F >

Minimal scope guard with move transfer and explicit release.

An armed guard calls its callable when it goes out of scope. Moving a guard transfers that responsibility and disarms the source. Calling release() disarms the guard. The callable must be nothrow-invocable.

FA nothrow-invocable callable type.
