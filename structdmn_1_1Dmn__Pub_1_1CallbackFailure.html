template<typename T = std::string, template< class > class QueueType = Dmn_BlockingQueue_Mt>
struct dmn::Dmn_Pub< T, QueueType >::CallbackFailure

A recorded exception from a filter or subscriber notification.

The exception can be inspected by rethrowing m_exception. Failures are recorded in callback invocation order and remain available until drained with takeCallbackFailures().
